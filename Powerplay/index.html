ⓘ Information & Tips

If you want to find out the system's % go to inara.cz then search the system and you'll see the % and the State. E.G Exploited 25.6%

Note: Post-4× multiplier values. Control Points per delivery = Merits ÷ 4.

This page does remember what you have typed in to it so if you dont want it to remember the system name click clear form button.

Import Info: use the “Import Info” button in the Current Inventory section to auto-fill your Data and on-foot Power Goods counts from your game's ShipLocker.json file, instead of typing them in by hand.

You'll find ShipLocker.json in your Elite Dangerous saved games folder, alongside your Journal log files:
C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Saved Games\Frontier Developments\Elite Dangerous\

The game rewrites this file live, so grab the latest copy while docked or after visiting your ship locker/backpack. Only your own items (not a crewmate's) are imported — anything picked up by another CMDR riding in your ship is skipped automatically so it doesn't skew your totals.

Share Link: use the “Share Link” button up top to copy a URL that pre-fills this page with your system, score, mode, and inventory — handy for sending to squadmates.
